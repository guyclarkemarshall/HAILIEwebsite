What happens next

  1. We acknowledge it. You will get a short email confirming we have received the document.
  2. A volunteer reviews it. We check it is relevant to housing and AI, and that it contains no personal data.
  3. We follow your instructions. Anonymised versions are sent to you for sign-off before anything is shared.
  4. It helps the sector. Your document informs guidance, templates and sessions for housing peers.

Documents we welcome

  • AI, data and acceptable use policies
  • DPIAs and risk assessments for AI tools
  • Governance and assurance frameworks
  • Board and committee papers on AI
  • Procurement questions and vendor checklists
  • Pilot and experiment write-ups

Please check before uploadingRemove tenant, staff or other personal data, and anything commercially confidential your organisation has not agreed to share. HAILIE is a volunteer community and cannot accept documents containing personal data.

Common questions

Sharing documents with HAILIE

Initially, only the HAILIE Chair and the small group of volunteers who review contributions. Beyond that, nothing is shared unless the option you chose on the form allows it. If you picked "internal use only", the document itself is never circulated.

Yes. Email guy@housingai.org and we will update or withdraw the document. The one exception is material already published under CC BY-SA 4.0, where copies others have made cannot be recalled, although we will remove it from housingai.org.

We remove your organisation's name, logos, author names, internal system names and any detail that would identify you, then send you the redacted version to approve before it is shared. You can also do the anonymising yourself before uploading if you prefer.

The form accepts a single PDF or Word document up to 10 MB. For anything else, including spreadsheets, slide decks or multiple files, email guy@housingai.org with the same information the form asks for, especially how we may use it.
